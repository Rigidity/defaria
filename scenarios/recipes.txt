# Recipe machines: a combustion generator burns logs into power for two
# electric furnaces over a copper wire, in a closed stone room so only the
# machines light it. One furnace smelts iron ore into ingots, the other
# sand into glass; each glows while it works and goes dark when it's done.
# `put` fills the machines as tubes would; `inspect RecipeProgress` shows
# each one's recipe, progress and status.
# Run: cargo headless scenarios/recipes.txt
camera 30 43 35 0 -12                                     # inside the room, looking north
settle                                                    # so it's loaded, whatever the radius
wait 5
fill 18 40 18 42 52 40 stone                              # the room's walls, floor and roof
fill 19 41 19 41 51 39 air                                # and the air inside
# All three face south, toward the camera; wires join their backs.
fill 27 41 30 27 41 30 electric_furnace[facing=south]
fill 30 41 30 30 41 30 combustion_generator[facing=south]
fill 33 41 30 33 41 30 electric_furnace[facing=south]
fill 27 41 29 33 41 29 wire                               # along their backs
wait 2
put 30 41 30 fuel 4 log                                   # four oak logs: a minute of 1 kW
put 27 41 30 input 4 stone[ore=defaria:iron]              # 4 s and 2 kJ each
put 33 41 30 input 4 soil[soil=defaria:sand]              # 3 s and 1.5 kJ each
wait 60
inspect RecipeProgress                                    # all three running
network 30 41 29                                          # about 1 kW produced (the furnaces started under a second ago), all of it used
screenshot target/scenarios/recipes/0-working.png
wait 1
camera 30 47 34 0 -50                                     # from above, the wire behind them
wait 2
screenshot target/scenarios/recipes/1-above.png
wait 1
camera 30 43 35 0 -12
wait 1200                                                 # 20 s: every ore and the sand done
inspect RecipeProgress                                    # the furnaces idle, the generator charging
inspect Slots                                             # four iron ingots and four glass
screenshot target/scenarios/recipes/2-done.png
wait 1
