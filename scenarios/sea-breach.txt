# Benchmarks flow on a sea. First the sea settled, which should cost
# nothing; then a wide cut from the land down to the bottom of the sea,
# which the sea pours into, lowering it a little. `timing`, `fluids` and
# `inspect FluidStats` every second show the frame times, how much is
# active, and that the water's total stays the same while nothing loads.
# Run: cargo headless scenarios/sea-breach.txt
camera 2866 50 70 0 -30
settle
timing
wait 120
timing
inspect FluidStats
fluids
screenshot target/scenarios/sea-breach/0-before.png
# `fluids` scans the whole loaded world, so start timing again after it.
wait 1
timing
