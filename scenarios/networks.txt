# Networks: a pipe line with a branch, a glass item tube beside it and a
# copper wire beyond, on a stone platform. Each kind is its own network,
# printed with `network`; the pipe and tube touch but don't join. Then
# the middle of the pipe line is broken, splitting it in two, and both
# halves are printed again; a pipe put back joins them into one.
# Run: cargo headless scenarios/networks.txt
camera 28 50.5 37.5 0 -40                                 # south of it, looking down

wait 5

fill 22 46 26 34 46 26 wire                               # a wire

wait 1
