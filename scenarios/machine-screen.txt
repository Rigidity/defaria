# Machine screens: a combustion generator burning logs powers an electric
# furnace smelting iron ore, in a closed stone room. Right click on the
# furnace opens its screen mid-recipe: its input and output slots, its
# progress and status, its energy, and the player's inventory under it.
# Shift-clicking its input takes the ore left there to the player, and a
# click on the player's sand puts one in. E closes it; then the
# generator's screen shows its fuel slot, its lava tank and its energy,
# with their numbers under the bars, and its inventory list scrolls. Last,
# a crate's nine slots are three by three, and the panel's top stays put.
# Run: cargo headless scenarios/machine-screen.txt
camera 30.5 41.5 33.5 0 0                                 # in front of the furnace, looking north
settle                                                    # so it's loaded, whatever the radius
wait 5
fill 25 40 26 37 46 36 stone                              # the room's walls, floor and roof
fill 26 41 27 36 45 35 air                                # and the air inside
fill 30 41 30 30 41 30 electric_furnace[facing=south]
fill 33 41 30 33 41 30 combustion_generator[facing=south]
fill 30 41 29 33 41 29 wire                               # along their backs
fill 27 41 30 27 41 30 crate
wait 2
put 33 41 30 fuel 8 log
put 33 41 30 fuel 2.5 lava
put 30 41 30 input 8 stone[ore=defaria:iron]              # 4 s each
put 27 41 30 items 64 log
put 27 41 30 items 64 planks
put 27 41 30 items 20 stone
wait 300                                                  # one ingot done, the next under way
hold MouseLeft 1                                          # lock the cursor
wait 1
hold MouseRight 1                                         # use the furnace
wait 3
inspect MachineScreen
