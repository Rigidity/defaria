# Double and vertical slabs, on a flat stone platform built in the air.
# Front row, west to east: a bottom slab, a top slab, then double slabs
# of one block (stone on stone) and of two (stone under planks, planks
# under thatch, granite bricks under birch). Back row: vertical slabs
# standing alone, facing each way, then pairs of them back to back, of
# one block and of two. A double slab next to a full block shows no
# seam where they meet, and a wall of them shades like a full wall.
# A slab in a pool keeps the water in its other half; a double slab
# beside it pushes the water out.
# The last views place and mine: a planks slab dropped onto the open top
# of a cobblestone one fills it, and mining the top half of a double slab
# leaves the bottom.
# Run: cargo headless scenarios/double-slabs.txt
wait 5
fill 20 45 20 44 45 44 stone       # the platform

fill 30 46 24 30 46 24 planks_slab[half=bottom]+thatch_slab[half=top]
fill 32 46 24 32 46 24 stone_brick_slab[half=bottom,rock=defaria:granite]+planks_slab[half=top,wood=defaria:birch]
fill 33 46 24 33 46 24 stone       # a full block against the last one
# Back row, along z = 28: single vertical slabs, one of each facing.
fill 22 46 28 22 46 28 stone_vertical_slab[half=north]
fill 24 46 28 24 46 28 stone_vertical_slab[half=south]

# A two-high wall of double slabs, its far end against stone.
fill 22 46 32 28 47 32 stone_slab[half=bottom]+planks_slab[half=top]
fill 29 46 32 29 47 32 stone
# A pool walled in stone: a slab in it, a double slab beside it.
