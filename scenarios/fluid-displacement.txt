# Blocks placed into fluid push it aside rather than delete it, sideways
# first: dirt dropped into a pond whose top layer isn't full pushes its
# water out through the pond on that level, raising the layer evenly, with
# nothing piled on top. Only with no room on its level, as in a pond
# filled to the brim, does the water go up, and it runs out over the
# bank, a thin film, instead of the pond draining. A block
# placed deep in a full, sealed pool has nowhere to push its water, so
# that's deleted and counted (`deleted`). A stone in a trench that isn't
# full pushes its water to the sides, raising the trench. And filling a wide stretch of a
# deep pool at once only wakes the water around each block: the `timing`
# after it stays close to the one before.
# `fluids` shows the totals: the ponds' water is all there after the dirt.
# (A `camera` jump loads and unloads chunks, which changes the totals.)
# Run: cargo headless scenarios/fluid-displacement.txt
camera 30 56 44 0 -38
settle

# to the brim: its surface is level with the bank around it.

# rows of water poured into the top one that spread over it.

# A trench in the bank, three wide, water half up it.
