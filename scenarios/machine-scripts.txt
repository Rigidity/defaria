# Scripted machines: solar panels (scripts/solar_panel.luau) make power
# from the sky light on their tops. On a wooden platform in the open, the
# front panel feeds three powered lamps along a copper wire, and they
# light; the back one is roofed over with a stone block, makes nothing,
# and its lamp stays dark. A third, under a roof three blocks up, gets
# less sky and makes a little less. `network` prints what each line
# produced and `inspect BlockData` what each panel counted in its data.
# Run: cargo headless scenarios/machine-scripts.txt
camera 30 50.5 40.5 0 -25                                 # south of it, looking north
settle                                                    # so it's loaded, whatever the radius
wait 5
fill 20 45 20 40 45 36 planks                             # the platform
fill 20 46 20 40 54 36 air                                # cleared above it
# In the open: a panel, a wire east and three lamps on it.
fill 23 46 31 23 46 31 solar_panel
fill 24 46 31 33 46 31 wire
fill 26 47 31 26 47 31 powered_lamp
fill 29 47 31 29 47 31 powered_lamp
fill 32 47 31 32 47 31 powered_lamp
# Roofed over: a panel with a stone block on it, a wire and a lamp.
fill 23 46 25 23 46 25 solar_panel
fill 23 47 25 23 47 25 stone
fill 24 46 25 27 46 25 wire
fill 26 47 25 26 47 25 powered_lamp
# Under a roof three blocks up, on its own.
fill 36 46 28 36 46 28 solar_panel
fill 35 49 27 37 49 29 stone
wait 120
network 28 46 31                                          # the open line: produced and consumed
network 25 46 25                                          # the roofed line: nothing produced
inspect BlockData                                         # each panel's count
inspect MachineActive

wait 1
camera 29 54 37 0 -55                                     # from above
wait 10
